Pocket Planetarium

A live map of the real sky above you. Drag to pan and pinch to zoom, or tap Point at sky to aim your phone at the real sky. The center is straight up (the zenith); the ring is your horizon, marked N, E, S, W. Stars are colored by their true surface temperature.

Star positions use the Yale Bright Star Catalog; constellation figures come from Stellarium (via the open-source astroterm project); planet and Moon positions use orbital elements from NASA JPL. Weather is from Open-Meteo, which aggregates national weather-service data.

Point-at-sky uses your phone's motion sensors and needs a compass; it can drift and may need a nudge with the calibrate arrows. Times use your device clock; positions are computed in UTC so they are correct wherever you set the location.
